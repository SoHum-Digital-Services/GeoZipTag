How location is used

Your coordinates are sent to Photon and OpenStreetMap's Nominatim to look up the address, and to Esri for satellite imagery. In the Android app, the phone's built-in address service is also asked. Photos stay on this device until you save or share them. Address data © OpenStreetMap contributors.

How to use

Take a stamped photo

Point the camera and press the shutter. The photo is stamped with the place, address, satellite map, coordinates, date and time, and saved straight away.

Choose a stamp

Satellite adds the map, address and GPS. Date & time stamps only the time, with no location. QR code adds a code that opens the spot in a map.

Location

The pin button at the top shows the GPS status. Wait for the green dot for the most accurate address, or set a location by hand.

Camera

Tap the screen to focus, pinch or use .5 / 1 / 2 to zoom, and use the buttons at the top for the grid, timer and torch. Photo size and quality are in Settings.

Your photos

Tap the thumbnail beside the shutter to open the last photo, share it, or delete it. Swipe down to go back to the camera.

Reports

The report button at the top turns a photo, or a before-and-after pair, into a PDF with your company logo and notes.

Privacy policy

GeoZipTag has no accounts, no ads and no tracking. Your photos stay on your phone.

Camera and photos

The camera is used only to take your photos. Photos are saved on your phone, in the GeoZipTag album or folder, and are shared only when you choose to share them.

Location

Your location is used to write the place, address and coordinates on your photos. To find the address and satellite map, the coordinates are sent to these services: Photon (komoot), Nominatim (OpenStreetMap), Esri satellite imagery, and your phone's own address lookup (Apple or Google). They receive only the coordinates, never your photos.

Reports

Reports, logos and notes are made on your phone and are not uploaded anywhere.

Updates

The app checks GitHub Pages for signed updates. This request contains no personal information.
