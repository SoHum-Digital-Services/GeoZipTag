How to use

Take a stamped photo

Point the camera and press the shutter. The photo is stamped with the place, address, satellite map, coordinates, date and time, and saved straight away.

Choose a stamp

Satellite adds the map, address and GPS. Date & time stamps only the time, with no location. QR code adds a code that opens the spot in a map.

Location

The pin button at the top shows the GPS status. Wait for the green dot for the most accurate address, or set a location by hand.

Camera

Tap the screen to focus, pinch or use .5 / 1 / 2 to zoom, and use the buttons at the top for the grid, timer and torch. Photo size and quality are in Settings.

Your photos

Tap the thumbnail beside the shutter to open the last photo, share it, or delete it. Swipe down to go back to the camera.

Attendance

Add your employee ID (and your team's) in Settings > Employee IDs, by typing them or importing a text, CSV or PDF list. Every photo then shows the ID with Log in or Log out. Tap the ID button at the top to roll to someone else's ID or switch between Log in and Log out. Settings > Attendance register lists every entry and exports a CSV for Excel.

Shared register

In Settings > Shared register a manager starts a team (a name and a 6-digit PIN) and gets a team code. Everyone else enters that code on their phone. Attendance photos are then sent to the team's online register, with a small copy of the photo, and wait on the phone while it is offline. The manager opens the register with the PIN to see everyone's entries and export them.

Reports

The report button at the top turns a photo, or a before-and-after pair, into a PDF with your company logo and notes.

Privacy policy

GeoZiptag by SoHum Digital Services has no accounts and does not track you. Your photos stay on your phone.

Camera, microphone and photos

The camera is used only to take your photos and videos, and the microphone only for the sound of videos you record. They are saved on your phone, in the app's own album or folder (named GeoZipTag), and are shared only when you choose to share them.

Location

Your location is used, only while the app is open, to write the place, address and coordinates on your photos. To find the address and satellite map, the coordinates are sent to these services: Photon (komoot), Nominatim (OpenStreetMap), Esri satellite imagery, and your phone's own address lookup (Apple or Google). They receive only the coordinates, never your photos.

Attendance

Employee IDs and the attendance register stay on your phone. Only if you join a team, each entry (ID, name, log in or out, time, place and a small copy of the photo) is also sent to that team's shared register, which only the manager PIN can open. Leave the team to stop.

Ads

The Android and iPhone apps show a small banner ad from Google AdMob in the photo view, never on the camera screen. Google may use your phone's advertising ID to show it. Your photos, location and attendance are never shared with the ad network.

Reports

Reports, logos and notes are made on your phone and are not uploaded anywhere.

Updates

The app checks GitHub Pages for signed updates. This request contains no personal information.
