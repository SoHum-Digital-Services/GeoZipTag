How location is used

Your coordinates are sent to Photon and OpenStreetMap's Nominatim to look up the address, and to Esri for satellite imagery. In the Android app, the phone's built-in address service is also asked. Photos stay on this device until you save or share them. Address data © OpenStreetMap contributors.
