GeoZiptag Privacy Policy

GeoZiptag by SoHum Digital Services · Last updated 25 November 2026

GeoZiptag is a camera app that writes the place, date, time and (optionally) an employee attendance entry onto your photos. It has no user accounts and no analytics, and we do not track you. The Android and iPhone apps show one small banner ad (see "Ads" below). Your photos stay on your phone unless you choose to share them or turn on the shared attendance register.

Camera, microphone and photos

The camera is used only to take the photos and videos you ask for. The microphone is used only while you record a video, for its sound. Photos and videos are saved on your phone, in the app's own album or folder (named GeoZipTag), and leave your phone only when you share them yourself.

Location

Your precise location is used, only while the app is open, to write the place, address and coordinates on your photos. To find the address and the map, the coordinates (never your photos) are sent to: Photon (komoot), Nominatim (OpenStreetMap), Esri satellite imagery, and your phone's own address lookup (Google or Apple). GeoZiptag does not track your location in the background.

Employee IDs and the attendance register

Employee IDs, names and the attendance register (log in / log out entries) are kept on your phone. You can export them as a CSV file, which you share yourself.

Shared online register (optional)

Only if you join a team with a team code, each attendance entry is also sent to that team's shared register so the team manager can see it. An entry contains: the employee ID and name, log in or log out, the date and time, the place, address and coordinates, and a small, low-resolution copy of the photo. It is stored on Supabase servers and can be read only by someone with the team's manager PIN. It is sent over an encrypted connection and is not sold or shared with anyone else. Leave the team in Settings to stop sending entries.

Reports, logos and notes

Reports, logos and notes are made on your phone and are not uploaded anywhere.

Ads

The Android and iPhone apps show a small banner ad from Google AdMob in the photo view, never on the camera screen. To show and measure ads, Google may use your device's advertising ID, IP address and app information, as described in Google's advertising policy. You can reset or limit your advertising ID in your phone's settings. Your photos, location, address and attendance details are never shared with the ad network. The web version has no ads.

App updates

The app checks GitHub Pages for signed updates to its screens. This request contains no personal information.

Deleting your data

Deleting a photo in the app, clearing the register, or uninstalling the app removes your data from the phone. To delete entries from a shared team register, ask your team manager, or contact us at geoziptag.sohum.cc with your team code and employee ID; we delete them within 30 days.

Children

GeoZiptag is meant for work use and is not directed at children under 13.
